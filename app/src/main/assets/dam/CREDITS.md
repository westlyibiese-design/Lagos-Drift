## Changes made for Lagos Drift
Cut down to the part around the dam (about 700 m x 330 m), see-through trees and sky removed, the terrain
and the small track details simplified to about 30,000 triangles, textures shrunk to 512 and 1024 pixels and
saved as JPG, scaled to 80 percent and baked into assets/dam/dam.bin. This is a modified version; the author
does not endorse Lagos Drift.

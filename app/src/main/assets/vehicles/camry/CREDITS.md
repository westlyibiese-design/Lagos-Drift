## Changes made for Lagos Drift
Converted from millimetres to metres, Y-up, forward = -Z, origin on the ground below the wheelbase centre.
Wheel geometry split into four nodes (wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr) with pivots at the wheel centres; the duplicated
wheel triangles (about 3,400) removed. The four doors (door_fl/fr/rl/rr) split into nodes of their own, each
with its handle, mirror, trim and window glass, and a pivot at the door's front edge (the hinge), so they can
swing open. Window glass made see-through. Body paint recoloured black. The small lamp textures were replaced
by their average colour and all colours packed into one tiny palette picture (palette.png) so the model is
cheap to draw. Baked into camry.car by a script. This is a modified version; the author does not endorse
Lagos Drift.

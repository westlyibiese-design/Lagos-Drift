## Changes made for Lagos Drift
Scaled to 0.868 of the original size, rotated so the nose points along -Z, origin on the ground below the middle
of the wheelbase. Wheels split into four nodes (wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr) with pivots at the wheel centres. The two
front doors (door_fl, door_fr) are nodes of their own with the hinge as pivot, and the front window glass moved
into them so it swings with the door. The light glow planes, the spare tyre on the roof rack and the unused
light textures were removed; the textures were resized to at most 1024 pixels. Window glass made see-through.
Baked into silverado.car by a script. This is a modified version; the author does not endorse Lagos Drift.

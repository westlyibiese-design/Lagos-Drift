## Changes made for Lagos Drift
Cut down to the cabin (the studio room, the lights and the rear of the cabin were removed), scaled and fitted
into the Camry (about 0.46 / 0.40 / 0.50 of the original size, nose along -Z, driver on the left), the glass
made see-through, and the geometry simplified from about 530,000 to about 46,000 triangles so it runs on
mid-range phones. Colours were set by hand. Baked into interior.car by a script. This is a modified version;
the author does not endorse Lagos Drift.
